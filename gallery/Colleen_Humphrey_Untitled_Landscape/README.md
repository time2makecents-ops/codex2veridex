# Colleen Humphrey — untitled landscape (title unconfirmed)

The photograph shows a signed landscape with a lower-left signature reading **Colleen Humphrey**. It appears to be an original watercolor or watercolor-based painting with substantial layered surface work; exact medium, support, title, date, dimensions, and value remain unverified.

Public Oregon records document Colleen Humphrey as a Portland watercolor artist. The Watercolor Society of Oregon listed her works at $900 (2006) and $1,100 (2012), but those figures are historical exhibition asking prices for different works and are not a valuation of this piece.

The Arts Council of Lake Oswego supplied a reference photograph of Humphrey's watercolor *Opening Night* in direct correspondence on September 16, 2026.
