# Sandy Brown Jensen — Trönninge Church, Sweden photograph (formal title unconfirmed)

The object is a framed interior photograph with a torn reverse studio label bearing `Sweden` and `Sandy Brown Jensen`. In private correspondence dated September 22, 2026, Jensen reviewed photographs of the object and confirmed that she made the image in **August 2018** at **Trönninge Church, Halmstad-Söndrum, Sweden**, during a family trip. She also confirmed that the surviving studio label is consistent with how she marked and prepared framed prints from this portfolio for gallery shows and exhibitions.

The artist attribution, capture date and location, travel context, and label consistency are directly confirmed. The formal title, photographic process and support, edition status, portfolio name, specific exhibition history, and ownership chain—including how the work reached the Seneca Goodwill—remain unconfirmed.

On September 23, 2026, Jensen explicitly said she did not know how the work reached Goodwill. She suggested preserving it with the broader Provenance Pending collection for a possible future gallery presentation. This is an expression of interest, not proof of provenance or a confirmed exhibition arrangement.
