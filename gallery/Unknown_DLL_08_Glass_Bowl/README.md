# Unknown maker - glass bowl or shallow platter marked "DLL '08" (reading uncertain)

## Object description

- Form: Large studio-glass bowl or shallow platter
- Approximate diameter: 13 inches (reported)
- Appearance: Primarily clear glass with trapped bubbles and textured geometric inclusions described as copper, bronze, blue, and purple
- Incised mark: Provisionally read as `DLL '08`; `DZL '08` or another reading remains possible
- Date: The mark may indicate 2008, but this has not been independently established

## Rejected attribution

David L. Lindsay reviewed an overall photograph and replied directly on September 23, 2026 that he did not make or recognize the piece. He explained that his work in 2008 was signed `Lindsay Art Glass` with the year. He also explained that `DLL` was used to identify his work during his 1976-1997 period with Nourot Glass and that most such pieces also bore a Nourot Glass mark.

This direct maker statement is strong evidence against attributing the object to David L. Lindsay. The item should remain listed under an unknown maker unless new documentary evidence establishes another identity.

Lindsay said the first message did not include a usable photograph of the inscription. The owner sent close-up photographs in a follow-up on September 23, 2026 and asked whether the mark might instead read `DZL`. No further response was present in the mailbox at the time of review.

## Other correspondence

- A representative of Gallery of Modern Masters replied on September 23, 2026 that they had no identification.
- Inquiries to Edgewood Orchard Galleries, Arts Benicia, and a Nourot-related contact had no replies in the mailbox as of September 23, 2026.

## Next evidence needed

- Straight-on macro photographs of the entire inscription under raking light
- Full top, bottom, side-profile, and polished-base photographs
- Exact diameter, height, weight, and base dimensions
- Documentation of every etched, engraved, acid-stamped, or applied mark
- Comparison with signed examples from glass artists whose initials could plausibly read `DLL`, `DZL`, or similar

Do not market or catalog this object as David L. Lindsay or Nourot Glass based on the current evidence.
