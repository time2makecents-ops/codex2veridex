How to use iPhone Archive

Follow this order for a safe, reversible archive. You can leave transcription and cleanup for later.

01

Choose where the archive lives

Open Settings. Set the local original destination, then set Another drive to a different physical drive such as E:\Veridex iPhone Backup. Click Verify and save after each choice.

02

Add your iPhone or export folder

Open Sources. Choose iPhone USB camera when the phone is connected by cable, or choose an iCloud/export folder. Add the source, then click Scan.

03

Review before downloading

Use Inventory to search, filter by date or type, and select files. The plan defaults to keeping files on the phone after download.

04

Download two verified copies

Click Download selected. Keep Create and verify a second copy checked when you want deletion to become eligible.

05

Transcribe only when wanted

Audio is never transcribed automatically. Select archived audio in Inventory, then open Audio and start a transcription job.

06

Clean up last

Cleanup only reports exact duplicates. Deletion review stays locked until the item has a verified primary archive and independent second copy.

Safety rule

The app does not delete from the phone during a normal download. Deletion requires an explicit plan, two-copy verification, a preview, and a typed confirmation.
