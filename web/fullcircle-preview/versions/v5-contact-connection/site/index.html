A few things you may be wondering

Before we begin.

What does a full-service sale include?+

Our intended service includes organizing and preparing items, pricing, promoting the sale, and staffing and running it. The scope of your particular sale will be discussed with you.

What happens to items that don’t sell?+

We’ll discuss the next step with you. Haul-away of unsold items is an optional service when requested; the scope and terms need to be agreed beforehand.

Where will Full Circle work?+

Eugene and surrounding areas, initially focusing on communities within 25 miles of Eugene.

Our fee structure and consultation details are being finalized. This draft does not quote a commission, minimum fee, or estimated sale return.
