## UO visit procedure

- Contact SCUA first; do not bring the artwork unannounced.
- UO’s reading room procedures concern UO archival materials, not walk-in private-art appraisal.
- If SCUA agrees to examine the piece, arrange a specific visit or handoff and ask whether they want the framed work or photographs only.
- For archival research visits, register and request materials at least five business days in advance.
- Paulson Reading Room: Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.; non-flash personal photography is allowed.
