# Frank Nichols — research notes

## Physical evidence

The `frank_nichols` folder contains four views of a framed nature photograph showing sunflowers against a purple flowering field. The artwork is accompanied by a studio card naming **Frank Nichols** and stating that the image was photographed, color-corrected, and printed by the author. The card identifies:

**Impact Images Studio / Pacific Photographic Arts**  
1720 NE 64th Ave., Suite B  
Vancouver, WA 98661  
360-750-0800  
frank@si-forest.com

The care card is dated 12/4/06. It also labels the subject/category “In Nature Photography.”

## Public research

- **Instagram lead supplied by the owner:** [@frank_nichols](https://www.instagram.com/frank_nichols/). Instagram could not be fetched directly during this check because access was throttled, so ownership and account contents still need confirmation. The handle is consistent with the name but is not, by itself, proof that it is the same Vancouver photographer.
- **Facebook contact lead supplied by the owner:** [Impact Images](https://www.facebook.com/impactimages). The owner reports sending a message to this page on September 18, 2026. Delivery, page ownership, and any response remain unverified.
- [Frank Nichols profile on Model Mayhem](https://www.modelmayhem.com/impactimages) identifies him as a Vancouver, Washington photographer and instructor of photography and Photoshop for the Pacific School of Photographic Arts. The profile reports Professional Photographers of America certification and awards claimed for 2006–2012; these credentials are self-reported and should be independently confirmed.
- Searches found no reliable current artist website or museum/auction record clearly tied to this specific photograph.
- A current website using the name Impact Images Studio appears to represent a different operation and should not be treated as Frank Nichols’ current site without confirmation.

## Attribution status

The physical card and signature/credit information make Frank Nichols the supported maker attribution for this photographic print. The exact title, edition status, print date beyond the 2006 card, dimensions, and current market value remain unverified.

## Best next step

Contact the phone number or email printed on the original studio card. Ask whether the studio can confirm the image title, print/edition details, date, and whether the studio or photographer still maintains records of the work.
