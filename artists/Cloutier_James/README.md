# James Cloutier

Research and correspondence for the Eugene, Oregon artist. Keep artist-level biography, contact attempts, and signature references here; keep individual artwork evidence in `gallery`.

## University of Oregon correspondence

On September 16, 2026, a University of Oregon Special Collections reference archivist confirmed that UO holds a small James Cloutier photograph collection consisting mainly of prints connected with his book on Alpine, Oregon. The archivist pointed to the SCUA finding aid and the digitized James Cloutier collection in Oregon Digital, said the available collection contains nothing from his Peace Corps period, and suggested Historic Oregon Newspapers for further local research. The archivist also noted a public LinkedIn page but could not share private contact information.
