# Marcy Baker — research notes

**Sources:** Marcy Baker Studio CV and contact pages, plus a 2017 artist interview and regional arts coverage. The uploaded `Artist research (1).docx` is retained as a lead document; its object-specific claims remain provisional.

Marcy Baker’s official CV lists a BFA from Syracuse University (1982), solo and group exhibitions from the 1990s through 2026, Print Arts Northwest affiliation, and collections including Hallmark Art Collection, Oregon Health & Science University, George Fox University, Pacific University, and others. It documents Santa Fe exhibitions and `Monothon 10` at Site Santa Fe in 1996.

Her official contact page currently lists Ithaca, New York, `info@marcybaker.com`, `studio@marcybaker.com`, and 503-775-2038. Her official website presents her practices as painting, collage, and printmaking and links to Facebook and Instagram.

The uploaded document identifies a work titled *Shadow Fish 9* as a monotype signed `MBaker '94`, reportedly accompanied by a Lightside Gallery label and studio business card from Santa Fe. The supplied artwork photographs independently confirm these core object details: the front is titled `Shadow Fish 9` and signed `M Baker '94`; the reverse label identifies the artist as `Marcy Baker`, the title as `Shadow Fish 9`, and the type as `monotype`. The label also records an original price of `$125` and a gallery inventory number. I found no authoritative online listing for the exact title.

Independent sources support her printmaking, collage, painting, teaching, Santa Fe-to-Portland career path, and monotype practice. A 2017 interview explains monotyping as a single print made by applying ink to a plate and transferring it to paper, and discusses her move from Santa Fe to Portland and later work in painting and collage. Regional coverage also documents her teaching monotype and collage at Multnomah Arts Center.

**Verification status:** Artist identity and career background are well supported. The title, date, signature, gallery provenance, and value estimates for *Shadow Fish 9* remain provisional until confirmed from the object, a contemporaneous record, or the artist.

**Object-photo verification:** The title, artist name, `M Baker '94` signature/date, monotype designation, Lightside Gallery label, original price, and inventory-number field are visibly present in the supplied photographs. This verifies that the document’s description matches the photographed object; it does not by itself prove authorship or current market value.
