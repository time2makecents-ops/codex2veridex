# Sandy Brown Jensen — research notes

**Source:** `Artist research (1).docx` (uploaded document; claims require independent verification).

## Direct artist confirmation

In private correspondence dated September 22, 2026, Sandy Brown Jensen reviewed photographs of the object and confirmed that she made the image in **August 2018** at **Trönninge Church, Halmstad-Söndrum, Sweden**. She explained that the visit occurred during a family trip and that relatives took her to the church on a day excursion.

Jensen also confirmed that the surviving studio label and fragment on the reverse are consistent with how she marked and prepared framed prints from this portfolio for gallery shows and exhibitions. The correspondence is retained in the owner's Provenance Pending Gmail account; the private message and contact details are not reproduced here.

The document identifies Sandy Brown Jensen as an Eugene, Oregon photographer, writer, and arts reviewer. It describes a `glomisé`-style process involving gilding the reverse of transparent photographic materials and references themes including landscapes, ravens, mythology, and Scandinavian/European travel.

The document mentions KLCC’s `Viz City`, an Artwork Archive profile, a Mind on Fire blog, and a Substack titled `That Mystic Road`. It also includes purported direct contact details and gallery references; verify all contact information against the artist’s current official pages before using.

**Verification status:** The artist attribution, August 2018 capture date, Trönninge Church location, Swedish travel context, and consistency of the reverse studio label are now confirmed directly by the artist. The formal title, photographic process and support, edition status, portfolio name, specific exhibition history, and ownership chain remain unconfirmed.

## Follow-up correspondence

In direct correspondence dated September 23, 2026, Jensen said she does not know how the photograph reached the Seneca Goodwill. She encouraged the owner to retain the photograph as part of a possible future exhibition of the Provenance Pending project, its objects, and their stories. She mentioned the upstairs gallery at Epic Seconds as one possible venue and offered to cover a future show. These comments document her support for the project; they do not establish the photograph's ownership chain or an exhibition commitment.

The owner subsequently sent Jensen photographs and brief descriptions of works attributed to Colleen Humphrey, James Cloutier, and Anja McVickar, asking whether she recognized McVickar. No response to that question was present in the mailbox as of September 23, 2026.

**Object-photo observations:** The supplied framed photograph depicts the interior of Trönninge Church with benches/pews and strong window light. A torn reverse label visibly includes `Sweden` and the name `Sandy Brown Jensen`. The exact title, photographic process, edition status, and provenance are not visible in the supplied images.

**Important distinction:** The document’s claims about gilded photography and specific series such as `Violet Hours` describe Jensen’s broader practice, but the photographed object does not visibly establish that this particular piece is gilded, unique, or part of one of those series.
