# Colleen Humphrey — research notes

## Identification

The photograph `humphrey.JPG` visibly shows a lower-left signature reading **Colleen Humphrey**. The work appears to be an original, heavily worked watercolor or watercolor-based painting on paper or a flexible support; the image alone cannot establish the exact medium or support.

## Public-record matches

- The Arts Council of Lake Oswego permanent-collection catalog lists a Portland artist named Colleen Humphrey and records her work *Opening Night* as watercolor, signed lower right.
- The Watercolor Society of Oregon's 2006 Aqueous Media Exhibition catalog lists **Colleen Humphrey, Portland — *Fruit, Fresh Fruit*, 22 x 30, $900**.
- The Society's 2012 exhibition catalog lists **Colleen Humphrey, Portland — *Show Stoppers*, 22 x 30, $1,100**.
- A 2011 Village Gallery Arts newsletter lists Colleen Humphrey among artists participating in a Portland-area featured-artist display.

## Direct institutional correspondence

On September 16, 2026, the Arts Council of Lake Oswego replied to the owner's inquiry and supplied a reference photograph of Humphrey's *Opening Night* from the City collection. The Council described that work as a watercolor, said its records contain no biographical or gallery-representation information, and referred the owner to Touchstone Gallery's archived Humphrey material. The supplied reference image is retained in the owner's email account.

The Council's email describes the signature on *Opening Night* as lower left, while the previously consulted public collection record was summarized as lower right. Treat signature placement as unresolved until the reference image and current catalog entry are compared directly. This discrepancy does not by itself weaken the documented existence of a Portland watercolor artist named Colleen Humphrey.

An inquiry to the Watercolor Society of Oregon and a follow-up inquiry to Touchstone Gallery had no reply in the mailbox as of September 23, 2026.
