## What is documented

The reference photograph shows a small figurative illustration with a visible lower-right mark that appears to read `Sy`. The photograph alone does not identify the maker, establish the medium, or establish a date.

Reliable biographical sources identify Sophie Helen Wisocky (1917–2018) as a Wisconsin resident/farmer and as the sister of Sister Josephine Cherubim Czahor, OSF. They do not identify her as an exhibiting or documented artist:

## Attribution status

The uploaded document's claim that the `Sy` signature belongs to Sophie Helen Wisocky is **not verified**. Searches performed found no reliable artist file, exhibition record, catalog entry, or comparable signed work tying Sophie to this drawing. This absence is not proof that she never made art; it means the attribution should not be presented as established.

Until family provenance or a matching signed example is found, catalog the object as **SY — unknown artist** and retain Sophie Wisocky only as a separate, unverified research lead.
